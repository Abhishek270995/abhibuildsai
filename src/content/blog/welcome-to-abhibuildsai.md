I'm Abhishek — a data analyst and AI builder with 6.5+ years across ad-tech, ed-tech, and digital commerce. I'm starting this newsletter for a simple reason: most of what I learn on the job never leaves the job. A good forecasting trick, a hard-won lesson about why a "simple" data pipeline broke, a small AI tool that saved someone three hours of manual work — that knowledge usually just sits in a Slack thread or a closed ticket.

This is where I'll write it down instead.

## What to expect here

**Data quality and SSOT systems.** I spend a lot of my time making sure the numbers a business relies on are actually correct — I'll share the patterns, tools, and mistakes I run into building monitoring and reconciliation systems.

**Revenue analytics and forecasting.** Practical notes on pipeline forecasting, KPI governance, and dashboarding — the kind of thing that's obvious in hindsight but took me a while to learn the first time.

**Applied AI, without the hype.** I'm building lightweight AI tools that solve narrow, real problems — including one running live on this site's [Tool page](/tool). I'll write about what actually works, what doesn't, and why "AI agent" doesn't have to mean "complicated."

## Why weekly

Short and consistent beats long and occasional. My goal is one useful, specific idea per post — not a monthly essay you'll bookmark and never read.

If that sounds useful, stick around. And if you're working on a data or AI problem yourself, I'd genuinely like to hear about it — [my inbox is open](mailto:abhiibuildsai@gmail.com).
